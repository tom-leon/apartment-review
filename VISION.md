# Product Vision

## Growth & UX Model
Free-to-browse, login-to-post. All reviews are public to maximize SEO. Submitting a review requires an account to prevent spam.

## Privacy Architecture
Require social SSO (Google/Apple) for verification, but strictly decouple user data from public display.

## Anonymity Tactics
Use broad attribution (e.g. "Lived here in 2023") instead of exact lease dates, so landlords can't identify tenants.

## Legal Mitigation (Defamation)
Structure UX prompts to focus on the property (structural issues, mold, etc.) rather than naming specific agents.

## Content Moderation
24-to-48-hour cooling-off period before a review goes live, to allow AI or manual screening for defamatory language.

## Ecosystem Balance
"Right of Reply" feature for property managers to respond — makes the site a balanced marketplace, not just a venting forum.
